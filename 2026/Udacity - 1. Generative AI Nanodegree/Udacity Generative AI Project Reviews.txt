Project 2 - NASA Mission Intelligence: Developing a TAG-based Chat System

Reviewer Note:

Congratulations on completing a very high-quality submission! You have built a sophisticated RAG system that not only retrieves and generates answers about NASA missions but also critically evaluates its own performance. Your implementation of the embedding pipeline and the integration of RAGAS metrics show a strong grasp of Generative AI engineering principles. Your documentation is exceptionally detailed, providing a clear roadmap of your development process and the hardware environment used.

3 Strengths

Robust Persona Engineering: Your NASA-expert system prompt is one of the strongest parts of the project, ensuring highly professional and grounded responses.
Sophisticated Async Handling: The way you managed asynchronous evaluation calls within the synchronous Streamlit framework is impressive and technically sound.
Comprehensive Metadata: Your data pipeline captures a wealth of information during ingestion, which greatly enhances the searchability and auditability of your vector store.

3 Improvements

Enhanced Data Sanitization: Implementing checksums or MD5 hashes for your text chunks would make your data ingestion pipeline more resilient to duplicate content.
Metric Expansion: Adding "Answer Correctness" to your evaluation suite would provide a more direct measure of whether the model is actually giving the right facts.
UI Tooltips: Adding small descriptions of the RAGAS metrics in the sidebar would make the system more accessible to users who aren't familiar with RAG evaluation.

Outstanding work on this multimodal moderation project! Your implementation demonstrates a solid understanding of:

Keep up the excellent work!
